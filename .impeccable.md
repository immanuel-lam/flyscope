## Design Context
### Users
A developer building reusable fruit fly brain research tools and later a connectome-constrained learning experiment.
### Brand Personality
Precise, tactile, exploratory. This is an instrument for inspecting neurons and activity.
### Aesthetic Direction
Provisional direction: dark scientific workspace; large 3D views, warm olive accents, quiet borders, readable compact controls. User was offered a style choice; this is the stated default pending feedback.
### Design Principles
- Keep the specimen and neuron geometry central.
- Always distinguish reconstructed anatomy from synthetic data.
- Keep activity provenance and coordinate units visible.
- Make imported projects use the same rendering contract.
- Provide keyboard-accessible controls and respect reduced motion.
